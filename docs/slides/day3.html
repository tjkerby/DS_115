Actual title: Data Scientist

What You Would Do

This position supports operational areas of the organization by using internal and external data to generate business insights, better understand member needs, and improve tactical and strategic decision-making.

You would:

  • Collaborate with business teams to extract analytical, behavioral, and predictive insights from data.
  • Apply statistical, programming, and analytical methods to business questions.
  • Design, develop, and maintain analytical and machine-learning models that will be put into operation.
  • Work across the full analytical lifecycle, including piloting models, A/B testing, data design, model deployment, and monitoring.
  • Explore internal and external data sources to identify useful patterns that were previously unknown to the business.
  • Develop models for problems such as customer churn, future value, and recommendation systems.
  • Build reporting tools around analytical models to measure their outcomes and business impact.
  • Run simulations, analyze trends, identify potential concerns, and perform statistical and financial analyses.
  • Explain analytical models and results to business users through presentations and training.

Technical Skills

  • Python and R
  • SQL
  • Linux
  • Relational databases, dimensional modeling, and data warehouses
  • Machine-learning methods such as linear regression, random forests, and support vector machines
  • Data mining and statistical analysis
  • Dashboard design and reporting
  • Model deployment and operationalization

Other Important Skills

  • Strong presentation and communication skills
  • Ability to explain technical models to nontechnical users
  • Ability to understand business needs and translate them into analytical solutions
  • Financial-services knowledge is helpful

Education & Experience

  • 3+ years developing and implementing analytical solutions
  • Master’s degree in a related field, or bachelor’s degree with equivalent experience

So what should we read in a job posting?

The title is a clue. The work is the evidence.

Look for the center of gravity: analysis, modeling, infrastructure, deployment, products, research, consulting, communication, or domain expertise.
